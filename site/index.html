    How the picks are made

    Each projection starts with the player's recent scoring, then adjusts for this week's opponent and game outlook. It's a simple, transparent model, not a crystal ball.

    • Form: this season's points per game, with recent games weighted more. Early in the season it's blended with last season's average so a few games don't swing it too far.
    • Matchup: how many points this week's opponent has allowed to the position compared with the league average. Defenses with only a few games are pulled toward average. Capped at ±20%.
    • Team total: the team's Vegas implied points for the game (from the over/under and spread). Higher-scoring games mean more fantasy points. Capped at ±15%.
    • Usage: whether the player's targets and carries (attempts and carries for QBs) over the last three games are up or down versus his season average. Capped at ±10%.

    Not included: injury reports, depth-chart changes, weather, or trades after the last update. Players on bye are left out, and players who haven't played in three weeks are dropped. Check injury news before you set a lineup.
